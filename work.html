Assay

Backtest verification for TradingView strategy builders. Upload a List of Trades export and get a letter grade with the reasoning: is the sample big enough to conclude anything, does the win rate clear what the reward-to-risk demands, was the worst losing run worse than chance predicts, and at what spread does the edge disappear. Free, no account, and the file you upload is never stored.

Edge Reality Check

A published TradingView indicator. From every bar it asks whether price travelled the target distance before the stop distance, and counts the answers — giving the hit rate random entry would have produced at a given reward-to-risk. That is the number any entry signal has to beat, and almost nobody knows theirs.
